Greek Sheet-Pan Chicken with Green Beans & Tomatoes

35 minserves 4 sheet-panBOGO proteinweeknight

Chicken breast, snipped green beans and Campari tomatoes roasted on one pan with Greek seasoning and plenty of olive oil. The tomatoes burst and make their own sauce.

  • Publix Pero Family Farms Snipped Green Beans Buy 1 Get 1 FREE

  • Publix Konriko Greek Seasoning Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Pompeian Imported Olive Oil Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Ben's Original Long Grain White Rice Buy 1 Get 1 FREE

From the pantry

  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Start the rice on the stove according to the package.
  2. Pound the chicken breasts to an even 3/4 inch (or halve them horizontally). Rub with olive oil, 1 1/2 tsp Greek seasoning, and the zest of the lemon.
  3. Toss green beans, whole Campari tomatoes, and sliced garlic with olive oil and a pinch of salt on a sheet pan. Nestle the chicken on top.
  4. Roast 20–25 min, until the chicken reaches 165°F and the tomatoes are blistered.
  5. Squeeze lemon over everything. Serve over rice and spoon the pan juices on top.

Buy two packs of chicken. Put the second pack in the fridge for the tacos below, or freeze it.

Chicken Tacos with Refried Beans & Salsa

30 minserves 4 tacosBOGO proteinkid-friendly

Uses the free pack of chicken: seared, spiced and shredded into crunchy taco shells, served with refried beans and salsa.

From the pantry

  • chili powder
  • ground cumin
  • garlic powder
  • 1 lime
  • shredded lettuce or cabbage
  • neutral oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and salt.
  2. Sear in an oiled skillet over medium-high, 6 min per side, until 165°F. Rest 5 min, then shred with two forks and toss with the lime juice and a spoonful of salsa.
  3. Warm the refried beans in a small pot with a splash of water. Warm the taco shells in the oven per the box.
  4. Dice the baby cucumbers for a cool, crunchy topping.
  5. Build the tacos: a smear of beans, then chicken, lettuce, cucumber, and salsa.

Rosarita Traditional refried beans may contain lard but have no dairy. The Vegetarian variety is also dairy-free. Old El Paso shells are normally dairy- and egg-free, but check the label on flavored varieties.

Italian Sausage Pasta with Marinara

25 minserves 4 pastaone-potweeknight

Browned Italian sausage simmered in jarred marinara and tossed with dry pasta. A pantry dinner where the protein, pasta, sauce and oil are all BOGO.

On sale this week

  • Publix Mueller's Pasta Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Classico Sauce Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Bertolli Olive Oil Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Campari Tomatoes Buy 1 Get 1 FREE

From the pantry

  • 1 onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • red pepper flakes
  • dried basil or Italian seasoning
  • salt

Steps

  1. Boil a big pot of salted water for the pasta (penne or rigatoni work well).
  2. Remove the sausage from its casings and brown it in a little olive oil in a deep skillet, breaking it up, about 6 min. Add the diced onion and garlic and cook 3 min more.
  3. Add a jar of marinara, a handful of halved Campari tomatoes, a pinch of pepper flakes, and the basil. Simmer 10 min.
  4. Cook the pasta 1 min shy of al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  5. Toss the pasta into the sauce, loosening it with the pasta water. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil. No cheese is needed.

Choose a Classico flavor with no cheese, like Marinara with Plum Tomatoes or Tomato & Basil. Skip the Four Cheese, Alfredo and Vodka varieties. Mueller's dry pasta is egg-free, but avoid any egg noodles. Carando sausage is usually dairy-free, but check the label because some Italian sausages include cheese.

Steps

Smoky Ham, Rice & Green Bean Skillet

35 minserves 4 one-panSouthernbudget

A one-skillet Southern-style dinner: seared ham steak, rice cooked in broth, and green beans, with a few dashes of Frank's RedHot.

On sale this week

  • Publix Pero Family Farms Snipped Green Beans Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Frank's Redhot Sauce Buy 1 Get 1 FREE
  • Publix Pompeian Imported Olive Oil Buy 1 Get 1 FREE

From the pantry

  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • smoked paprika
  • dried thyme
  • salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Dice the ham steak into 1/2-inch cubes. Brown in olive oil in a large lidded skillet over medium-high, 5 min. Remove.
  2. Add the diced onion and cook 4 min, then the garlic, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 1/2 tsp thyme for 30 sec.
  3. Stir in 1 cup rice to toast for 1 min, then add 2 cups Swanson broth. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 12 min.
  4. Scatter the green beans and ham over the rice, cover again, and cook 6–8 min more until the rice is tender and the beans are crisp-tender.
  5. Fluff, season, and shake Frank's RedHot over the top to taste.

The second ham steak and the extra broth, rice and green beans from the BOGOs can make this a second time later in the week, or go into a quick fried rice. Standard ham steak and Swanson broth are dairy-free, but check the label on flavored or glazed hams.
